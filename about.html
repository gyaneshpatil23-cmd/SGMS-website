About us

A valued partner to banks and borrowers.

Established in 2019 and registered as a private limited company in 2023, SGMS Online Solutions serves the banking industry and individuals from offices in Nashik and Pune.

Who we are

A service company built around the loan file.

SGMS Online Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a private-sector service provider for the banking industry and for individuals. Our work sits at the point where a loan meets its paperwork: equitable and registered mortgages, stamp duty challans, and the documents that follow disbursement.

A full-time team of eight handles every case from our Nashik and Pune offices, so banks and their customers have one dependable desk to turn to.

  • Equitable Mortgage (Notice of Intimation)
  • Registered Mortgage
  • Challan MTR Form No. 6 filling / e-SBTR
  • Documents collection — PDD & other
