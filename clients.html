Who we work for

Two kinds of client, one standard of work.

Banks & lenders

Bank and NBFC branches and loan teams hand us the mortgage documentation that has to be completed before and after disbursement, and get the filed papers back for the loan file.

  • Notice of Intimation for equitable mortgages
  • Registered mortgage deeds
  • MTR Form No. 6 challans and e-SBTR
  • PDD and other document collection

Individuals

Borrowers on a home or property loan come to us directly for the paperwork their lender asks for, so it is done correctly without repeat visits.

  • Filing the Notice of Intimation on your loan
  • Stamp duty and registration fee challans
  • Help with registration at the Sub-Registrar office
  • Filed copies and receipts for your records
